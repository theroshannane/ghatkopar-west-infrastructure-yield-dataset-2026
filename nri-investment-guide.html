NRI Real Estate Investment in Mumbai: 2026 Legal & Financial Framework

This briefing summarizes the core legal, FEMA, RBI, and tax mechanisms governing property acquisitions in Mumbai by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) residing across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Singapore, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

Official Treatise: Read the complete 15,000-word treatise published on the Emperia LBS 21 NRI Research Portal.

1. FEMA & RBI Acquisition Protocols

Under General Permission granted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) pursuant to the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), NRIs and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) may acquire unlimited residential and commercial properties in India without prior regulatory sanction.

2. Repatriation & Banking Architecture

  • NRE Accounts: Used for inwards remittance of foreign currency. Sales proceeds up to original remittance are fully repatriable.
  • NRO Accounts: Used for rental inflows. Rental income is repatriable under Form 15CA and 15CB certification.
  • Annual Limit: Up to USD 1,000,000 per financial year can be repatriated out of NRO accounts under RBI guidelines.
